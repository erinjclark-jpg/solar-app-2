Auditor's Note on Technical Valuation

The conclusion of value above represents an estimated **Fair Market Value** (FMV) derived from empirical technical asset evaluation standards. While the service provider may present a "payoff" figure based on the full value of a theoretical 20-year revenue stream, the market reality of used solar equipment attached to real property is governed by actual avoided utility costs and current replacement costs, adjusted for physical and functional depreciation observed onsite. Furthermore, the Cost Approach explicitly excludes unavailable tax incentives (such as the Federal ITC), and the Income Approach accounts for expected future capital expenditures verified during the technical audit.

Statement of Limiting Conditions
This Fair Market Value (FMV) audit represents an independent technical asset valuation based on the Principle of Substitution, current replacement costs, and discounted cash flow models. This document relies on empirical hardware inspection and metered system performance to ground financial assumptions in physical reality. It is not an appraisal governed by USPAP guidelines, but rather a technical engineering audit intended to facilitate fair market negotiations by establishing the verified operating condition of the system. While the data herein is verified by a licensed solar inspection professional, this document does not constitute a legal review of contractual obligations, nor does it guarantee buyout acceptance or specific settlement figures by third-party owners (TPOs). Assumed underwriting variables—including utility escalation caps, hardware lifecycle estimates, and tax incentive exclusions—are applied to simulate fair market conditions but may vary from actual future events. This report is not intended to serve as formal tax, legal, or actuarial counsel.
