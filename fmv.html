Appraiser Note on Valuation

The conclusion of value above represents the **Fair Market Value** (FMV) as defined by standard appraisal practices. While the service provider may present a "payoff" figure based on the full value of a 20-year revenue stream, the market reality of used solar equipment attached to real property is governed by actual avoided utility costs and current replacement costs, adjusted for physical and functional depreciation observed onsite.
